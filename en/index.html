Our standards

What we publish, and what we refuse to.

  • Every number has an address. Value, primary source, link, and the date we checked it.
  • Corrections are public. Find an error and we fix it, then send the new version to every reader.
  • No sponsors from finance. No bank, broker or fund pays to appear in a file.
  • No investment advice. We explain mechanisms. We don't recommend securities, issuers or institutions.
  • No manufactured urgency. No countdown timers, no “price goes up tonight,” no invented testimonials.
